# 📊 Employee Attrition Dynamics: Exploring Trends, Patterns, and Predictive Factors

This project investigates employee attrition using statistical and machine learning techniques to uncover key factors contributing to employee turnover. It aims to help organizations improve retention strategies by understanding patterns in job satisfaction, salary, performance, department-wise attrition, and more.

---

## 🧠 Project Overview

Employee attrition affects organizational efficiency, culture, and cost. In this report, we apply statistical analysis and predictive modeling to explore:

- Why employees leave organizations
- The role of salary, performance, tenure, and satisfaction
- Departmental differences in retention
- Key predictors of performance

The analysis leverages descriptive statistics, correlation analysis, regression modeling, and visual insights to draw actionable conclusions.

## 🔍 Key Analyses & Findings

1. **Attrition Rate Analysis**
   - ~16.1% of employees have left.
   - Indicates notable turnover requiring HR intervention.

2. **Tenure vs. Attrition**
   - Employees with more years at the company are less likely to leave.

3. **Salary vs. Attrition**
   - Weak negative correlation. Salary alone doesn’t strongly predict attrition.

4. **Departmental Differences**
   - Sales roles show highest attrition, especially Sales Reps.
   - Technology has a more stable workforce.

5. **Active Workforce Distribution**
   - Technology leads in active employees; HR has the fewest.

6. **Performance Prediction**
   - Top predictors of performance: Salary, Tenure, Job Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance.
